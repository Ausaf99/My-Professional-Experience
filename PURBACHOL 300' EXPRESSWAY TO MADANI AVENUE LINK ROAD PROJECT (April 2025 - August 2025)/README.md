# Purbachal 300' Expressway to Madani Avenue Link Road Project (Apr 2025 – Aug 2025)

This repository contains selected project materials related to the **Purbachal 300' Expressway to Madani Avenue Link Road Project**, financed by **Roads and Highway Department, Bangladesh** and implemented by the **Bangladesh Army**.

## My Role
I contributed as a **Planning & Design Engineer** at **GeoStruct Consultant Limited**, supporting the project team in:
- Road alignment review and planning support
- Geometric design elements ( U-turns, curves, islands, traffic control features)
- Quantity estimation / **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** support
- Documentation and presentation materials for submission

## Repository Contents
- **Design submitted to Bangladesh Army/**  
  Submission-ready documents (as included/allowed for sharing)

- **Rendered Images of Final design (10 Aug 2025)/**  
  Rendered visuals of the final design outputs

- **Site Visit/**  
  Photos and notes from field/site visits
